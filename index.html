Need to know

COMMON QUESTIONS.

What is Day1 Roleplay?

Day1 is an 18+ serious roleplay community focused on immersive, character-led stories and a welcoming player experience.

How do I join the server?

Join our Discord, complete the application process, and follow the instructions from the team once your application has been reviewed.

Do I need previous roleplay experience?

No. We value a thoughtful attitude and a willingness to learn. The rules and community will help you get started.

How long does an application take?

Review times can vary with application volume. Keep an eye on Discord and the player dashboard for your status.

What happens after I am whitelisted?

You can connect to the server, create your character, and begin finding your place in the city.
